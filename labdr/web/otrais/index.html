Welcome to Riga

Riga is the capital and largest city of Latvia. Located on the banks of the Daugava River near the Baltic Sea, it is known for its historic Old Town, Art Nouveau architecture, lively markets, and green parks. The city's historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Travel tip: Explore the Old Town on foot, then discover the quieter streets and parks beyond it.

Featured Attractions

Vecrīga — Riga's Old Town

Wander along cobblestone streets past historic churches, squares, and colourful buildings. Highlights include Riga Dome Cathedral, St. Peter's Church, and the House of the Black Heads in Town Hall Square.

Take time to explore the small streets and cafés. Learn more about the city at LiveRiga.

Art Nouveau District

Riga is famous for its Art Nouveau buildings. Around Alberta iela and Elizabetes iela, look up to see elaborate façades, sculpted faces, and decorative details from the early twentieth century.

For a closer look at this architectural style, visit the Riga Art Nouveau Centre on Alberta iela.

Riga Central Market

Near the central railway station, Riga Central Market occupies several large pavilions originally built using structures from former airship hangars. Browse fresh produce, baked goods, fish, and other local foods.

Try traditional Latvian rye bread or explore the seasonal fruit and vegetable stalls.

Practical Travel Tips

Useful information for visitors to Riga
Topic Information
Language Latvian is the official language.
Currency Latvia uses the euro (€).
Transport Riga has trams, trolleybuses, and buses.
Weather Bring layers and a rain jacket, especially outside summer.
Walking Comfortable shoes are useful on Old Town cobblestones.

Remember: Check opening hours and transport schedules before your visit.
